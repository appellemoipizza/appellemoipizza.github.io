# README.md

**Q: What is this blog?**

A: A small research journal about systems, experiments, and the reasoning behind the work. Research notes, build logs, unfinished experiments, and references worth keeping around.

**Q: How can I get an update when a new post comes out?**

A: There is an [RSS feed](/rss.xml). I also post about new entries on [Twitter](https://x.com/appellemoipizza). No newsletter, no tracking, no popups.

**Q: What tools do you use for the site?**

A: It is a static site built with Astro and deployed through GitHub Pages. Everything is dithered black and white on purpose. The images are processed with a custom ordered-dither script rather than a filter.

**Q: What if I see something incorrect in a post?**

A: Please tell me. Open an issue on [GitHub](https://github.com/appellemoipizza) or reach me on Twitter. Corrections are welcome and I would rather be right than look right.

**Q: Do you update your old posts?**

A: Yes, when something turns out to be wrong or when I learn more. Substantive updates get a dated note at the top of the post rather than a silent edit.

**Q: Can I translate your posts to another language?**

A: Yes, and gladly. Please email or message me in advance, and keep a link to the original post at the top of the translation rather than buried in small text at the bottom.

**Q: How often do you post?**

A: When there is something worth writing down. No schedule, because a schedule would mean publishing on days when nothing was learned.

**Q: Where is the source?**

A: [github.com/appellemoipizza](https://github.com/appellemoipizza). The repository is public, this page included.
