Loket is intentionally boring software for an expensive boring problem.

Professional firms repeatedly ask clients for documents, receive half of them through email or messaging apps, chase the missing pieces, rename files and reconstruct what happened. Loket gives the client a secure accountless link and gives the firm a structured dossier.

## Building the business around the product

The interesting work was not only the interface. The product forced decisions about passkeys, email deliverability, EU data regions, private file storage, payment webhooks, legal documents, billing boundaries, production/development separation and a prospecting system that cannot casually damage the product's sending domain.

It is the project that taught me how much of shipping software is really **designing boring operational contracts so future-you cannot make an expensive mistake by accident.**
