I've rebuilt this site four times in the last year and never once used it to write anything down. Cards, animations, a whole 3D flip interaction for the front page, and an empty notes folder behind it. That ratio finally started to bother me.

Here's the actual reason: I forget my own reasoning embarrassingly fast. I'll make a real decision, why a system checks its own work before trusting it, why a product should say nothing rather than say something plausible-but-unverified, and three weeks later, mid-conversation with someone, I'll reconstruct a worse version of the argument from scratch. The thinking happened. It just didn't happen anywhere I can go back to.

## What this is not

It's not a content calendar, and it's not going to be. I'm not writing to a schedule, optimizing for a following, or turning build logs into a personal brand. If that happens as a side effect, fine, but it's not the target and I'd rather have zero readers and honest notes than an audience and a performance.

## What it is

A record, kept close to when the decision actually happened. Something went a specific way for a specific reason, write it down before the reason gets sanded off into a clean origin story I'd tell later. The boring version, written now, is more useful to me than the flattering version, written in retrospect.

That means two kinds of entries will end up here, and they'll look nothing alike. Some are just me talking through why I built something a particular way. Others are closer to actual research, I track things, I write down what I measured, and I'd rather show the number than describe the vibe. I'm not going to smooth those into one consistent voice. A decision explained in a paragraph and a decision backed by four weeks of logged data are different kinds of claims, and I want the format to say so before you've read a word of either.

## The only rule

If I wouldn't stand behind a sentence in front of the person it's about, it doesn't get published. Everything else, how long, how technical, how finished, is negotiable.
